Delete your Lulara account

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in Lulara, the AI study and tutoring app, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Lulara.
  2. On the home screen, tap the menu icon (☰) in the top left corner.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, send an email to contact.buluterus@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Lulara account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its link to your Google sign-in
  • Your profile information (name or nickname, education level)
  • Your cloud backup: chats, notes and study plans
  • Your credit balance and Premium status record
  • Your invite code and invite records
  • Feedback you gave on AI answers and rewarded-ad records

There is no additional retention period: this data is removed from our servers when the deletion is carried out.

What is not deleted

  • Google Play purchase records: Subscription and payment records are kept by Google Play according to Google's own policies. We never receive or store your payment details.
  • Anonymous statistics: Daily usage counts (for example, the total number of chats per day) that are not linked to you.
  • Data on your device: Chats, notes and settings saved on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

Premium subscribers: Deleting your account does not cancel your Premium subscription. Please cancel it first in the Google Play Store app: Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Lulara → Cancel subscription.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual items directly in the app at any time, without deleting your account:

  • Chats: open the chat list in the Chat tab and delete the chat.
  • Notes: open the Notes tab and delete the note.
  • Study plans: in the Plan tab, swipe the plan to the side to delete it.

Deleted items are removed from your device and from your cloud backup immediately. There is no additional retention period.

You can also request deletion of specific data (for example, only your profile information or only your cloud backup) by emailing contact.buluterus@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will process your request within 30 days.
