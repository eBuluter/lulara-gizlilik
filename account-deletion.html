Delete Your Account — Lulara

Last updated: August 2026

You can request deletion of your Lulara account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how.

How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — From within the app:
Open Lulara, tap the menu icon in the top-left corner, tap "Contact us", and write a message asking us to delete your account. Please include the email address you signed in with.

Option 2 — By email:
Send an email to contact.buluterus@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", including the email address associated with your Lulara account.

What Gets Deleted

Once we process your request, the following data associated with your account is permanently deleted:

  • Your account information (name, email, profile photo)
  • Your chat history and any photos or documents you shared
  • Your quiz results, flashcards, learning plans, and progress data
  • Your credit balance and subscription status

Processing Time

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Data We May Retain

We may retain limited transaction records (such as purchase receipts) where required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes, even after account deletion. This data is not used for any other purpose.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this process, contact us at: contact.buluterus@gmail.com
